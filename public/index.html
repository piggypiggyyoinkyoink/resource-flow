Help and shortcuts

Choose a node and click the canvas to add it. Choose a connection type, then click its origin and destination. State connections can attach to another connection.

Drag empty canvas to select fully enclosed nodes and connection labels. Drag a selected node to move the selection. Alignment guides snap nodes into horizontal or vertical alignment.

Select a connection to edit its waypoints. Drag a waypoint to move it, or a small segment handle to add one. Double-click a waypoint to remove it. Nearby right angles snap into place. Hold Alt while dragging to position freely.

Drag a connection's endpoint handles to reconnect it. State links can also attach to another connection. An endpoint dropped on empty canvas stays disconnected and appears in Diagram issues.

In an interactive simulation, click an On click node to queue its action for the next step. Register values can also be adjusted in Properties.
